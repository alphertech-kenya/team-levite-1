Who We Are

Team Levite 1 is a youth team under Spirit Power Embassy.


We are a generation committed to knowing God, serving Him faithfully, walking in His presence and impacting our generation for Christ.

Our Vision

To raise a generation that knows God, walks in His truth, serves with passion, and boldly carries His presence wherever they go.

Our Mission

To equip young people through prayer, worship, the Word of God, fellowship and service, creating a generation that lives for God's purpose.
